Anhydrous (NH3) Price Map

Call or text your dealers for anhydrous quotes, type each one in as you get it, and this map keeps them all side by side — cheapest first, with the date you got each quote so you know what's gone stale. Prices are saved in this browser on this device.

Map

Blue = dealer with no quote entered yet, green = dealer you have a current quote for, orange = quote has gone stale, purple = Koch wholesale terminal (where the dealers load — not retail). Use the search box in the top-left of the map to jump straight to a dealer by name. Click any empty spot on the map to drop a pin for your farm (draggable) — then clicking a dealer also shows driving miles and time from your farm. The target button uses your current location instead. Dealers came from the Iowa Dept of Ag fertilizer-license list, which doesn't say who handles anhydrous specifically — the first call to each one tells you; delete pins that don't.
